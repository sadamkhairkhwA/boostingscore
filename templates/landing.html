FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a credit card to sign up?
No. The free plan needs no credit card and no setup. Create an account and start practising straight away.
Is the AI scoring accurate?
The scorer is trained on IELTS band descriptors across all four criteria for Writing and Speaking. Treat it as a clear guide for improvement — not an official exam score from Cambridge, IDP or the British Council.
Does it include Speaking practice?
Yes. In the practice test, examiner videos play, you record your answer, Whisper transcribes it, and AI scores you on the four IELTS speaking criteria. There is also a Speaking study section with questions, phrases, pronunciation notes and tips.
Is it for Academic or General Training?
Practice tests and the full reading papers are Academic-focused. Writing covers Academic Task 1 and Task 2. Listening and Speaking are shared across both modules. There is also a General reading area for broader fluency practice.
How long does it take to see improvement?
It depends on your starting level and how often you practise. Many people notice clearer structure and fewer repeated mistakes within a few weeks of short, regular sessions — especially when they review AI feedback and retry the same skill.
