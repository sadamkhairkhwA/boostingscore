Scanning — find information fast

Scanning means moving your eyes quickly to find one specific thing. You do not read every word — you search, like Ctrl+F on a computer.

🔍
Think of it like this
You are in a library looking for “The Great Gatsby”. You do not read every title. Your eyes move fast looking for G, then Ga, then Gatsby. You ignore everything else. That is scanning.
In IELTS, the question gives you a keyword. Your job is to find that keyword — or its synonym — in the passage. You do not need to understand the passage. You need to locate one piece of information.
