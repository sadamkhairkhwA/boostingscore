Skills lab

Short lessons you can complete in 5–10 minutes. Use “Reveal” to check understanding.

Scanning

Scanning means moving your eyes quickly to find a specific keyword or synonym — not reading every word.

The council introduced new parking rules near the station to reduce congestion during peak hours.
Try scanning for: parking, station, congestion, peak.

Skimming

Skim for structure: first sentences, headings, and repeated nouns. Goal: map the passage in 60–90 seconds.

Checklist: What is the topic? What changes between paragraphs? Where is the definition/example/contrast?

Paraphrase radar

IELTS questions rarely repeat passage wording. Train yourself to recognize synonyms and grammatical shifts.

Example: “reduce costs” ↔ “cut spending”, “since 2010” ↔ “for more than a decade”.

True / False / Not Given

  • True: matches the passage.
  • False: contradicts the passage.
  • Not Given: not mentioned (do not infer from “common sense”).

Yes / No / Not Given

These questions usually target claims or views. Treat “Not Given” the same way: if the passage does not address the statement, choose Not Given.

Speed without panic

Speed comes from decision rules: if a question burns more than 90 seconds, mark your best guess, flag it mentally, and move on.

Accuracy first on Parts 1–2; stamina for Part 3. Finish the test — unanswered questions always score zero.
