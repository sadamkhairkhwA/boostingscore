Guide

How spaced repetition and your personal deck work on this site.

  • Daily deck — Custom cards you have not mastered appear when they are “due”. Tap Reviewed on a card after you study it to schedule the next review (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days).
  • Mastered — Removes a custom card from rotation so you can focus on what is still hard.
  • Global words — Admin-added words are always available in the topic; only your own cards use the spaced schedule.
  • Word bank — Saving a new custom card also adds a line to your writing word bank for later reuse.
  • Definition pictures — When you create or edit your own card (or when an admin adds one for a global word), an optional image appears on the definition side in Flashcards and the word list.
